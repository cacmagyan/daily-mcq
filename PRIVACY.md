# Privacy Policy: CACMAGyan MCQ Poster

Last updated: 30 October 2026

CACMAGyan MCQ Poster is the tool that runs the Instagram page **@cacmagyan**. It posts study content (MCQs, concept notes and short videos for CA and CMA students) and answers people who ask for a concept note in the comments.

## What we use

When you comment on one of our posts asking for the notes (for example, "NOTES"), the tool reads:
- your comment's text,
- your Instagram username, and
- the comment's ID and time.

We use these only to send you the concept note you asked for as one Instagram direct message, and to reply "Sent! Check your DMs" under your comment.

We also read the public numbers of our own posts (views, likes, saves, shares, follower count) to see which posts help students most.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
- We don't send you any message you didn't ask for. You get one message per request.
- We don't keep a list of commenters. Your username is not stored after your request is answered. Instagram keeps your comment and our reply as usual.

## Your choices

- To get no message, simply don't comment the keyword.
- To have anything about you removed, delete your comment on Instagram, or send a message to **@cacmagyan** on Instagram asking us to delete anything related to you. We will do it within 7 days.

## Contact

Send a direct message to **@cacmagyan** on Instagram.
